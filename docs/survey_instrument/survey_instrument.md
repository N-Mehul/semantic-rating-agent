# Smartphone Technology-Adoption Survey Instrument

**Draft status:** Step 2 instrument draft for faculty/content review. Questions concern the respondent's current smartphone, meaning the smartphone they use most often now. No persona generation, LLM response generation, or synthetic data is part of this instrument.

## Response Scale

Use the same five response options for every agreement item:

1. Strongly disagree
2. Disagree
3. Neither agree nor disagree
4. Agree
5. Strongly agree

## Free-Text Responses

Place these before their corresponding construct Likert items. Paragraph responses are optional; remind respondents not to include names, phone numbers, account details, or other identifying information.

**FT_PE01 — Performance Expectancy**  
In your own words, how, if at all, does your current smartphone help you accomplish tasks or perform daily activities?

**FT_PV01 — Price Value (open-ended only)**  
In your own words, how do the benefits you receive from your current smartphone compare with what it costs?

**FT_BI01 — Behavioral Intention**  
What factors most influence whether you intend to continue using your current smartphone or choose a replacement?

## Performance Expectancy (PE)

PE01. My current smartphone helps me complete everyday tasks more effectively.

PE02. Using my current smartphone increases my productivity in activities that matter to me.

PE03. My current smartphone enables me to accomplish tasks that would otherwise be difficult.

PE04. Overall, my current smartphone is useful for the things I need to do.

## Effort Expectancy (EE)

EE01. Learning to use my current smartphone was easy for me.

EE02. The way to interact with my current smartphone is clear and understandable.

EE03. I have found it easy to become skillful at using my current smartphone.

EE04. I can usually complete tasks on my current smartphone without much effort.

## Hedonic Motivation (HM)

HM01. Using my current smartphone is enjoyable.

HM02. I have fun using my current smartphone.

HM03. Using my current smartphone is entertaining.

HM04. I find using my current smartphone pleasurable.

## Social Influence (SI)

SI01. People who are important to me think I should use my current smartphone.

SI02. People whose opinions I value support my use of my current smartphone.

SI03. My family or friends encourage me to use my current smartphone.

SI04. People around me whose technology use I respect use smartphones like mine.

## Perceived Protection (PP)

**Working interpretation for this draft:** perceived protection means perceived protection of personal information and accounts by the current smartphone. Confirm this definition with the faculty/research framework before fielding.

PP01. My current smartphone protects my personal information from unauthorized access.

PP02. I feel that my personal data are protected when I use my current smartphone.

PP03. The privacy and security controls on my current smartphone give me control over my information.

PP04. I feel safe using my current smartphone for activities involving personal information.

## Behavioral Intention (BI)

BI01. I intend to continue using my current smartphone for the foreseeable future.

BI02. I expect to use my current smartphone regularly over the next six months.

BI03. I plan to rely on my current smartphone for my everyday needs going forward.

BI04. When I next need a smartphone, I intend to choose one with capabilities similar to those I value in my current smartphone.

## Smartphone Aspect Satisfaction

AS_BAT01. The battery life of my current smartphone meets my needs.

AS_CAM01. The camera quality of my current smartphone meets my needs.

AS_PERF01. The speed and responsiveness of my current smartphone meet my needs.

AS_DES01. The design and physical feel of my current smartphone meet my preferences.

AS_DISP01. The display of my current smartphone meets my needs.

## Overall Satisfaction

SAT01. Overall, I am satisfied with my current smartphone.

## Construct-Specific Anchor Statements

The separate [smartphone_construct_anchor_statements.csv](smartphone_construct_anchor_statements.csv) contains five newly written statements for each of the six constructs, mapped to ratings 1–5. They are proposed domain-specific adaptations for future semantic scoring. **They are not Maier et al.'s original SSR anchors.** The original paper's six exact purchase-intent sets remain unavailable in this repository; see [the original anchor audit](../ssr_verification/original_anchor_source_audit.md).
