## 5. Limitations
1. **Sample size.** 20 synthetic responses (10 per persona) from one model, one run, temperature 1.0; 194 usable human Q26 answers, of which 102 belong to P1 and P2. Results are preliminary and can change with a different run.
2. **Coverage.** P3 and P4 were never generated (quota). Conclusions apply, at most, to the two tested personas as defined here.
3. **Single adapted anchor set.** Only the Behavioral Intention set (`PROPOSED_DOMAIN_ADAPTATION_NOT_ORIGINAL_MAIER_ANCHOR`) was used. The paper's six anchor sets could not be recovered and were not invented, so this is not a reproduction of the published method.
4. **Local encoders** (MPNet, MiniLM, BGE-base) instead of the paper's `text-embedding-3-small`; absolute E[r] levels and even the sign of differences depend on the encoder.
5. **Q21 is a proxy** for the construct the anchors describe; it is a single self-report item.
6. **Independently sampled profiles.** Synthetic profiles sample each attribute independently from the persona's marginals, so some combinations are unrealistic and correlations among attributes are lost.
7. **Prompt-shaped output.** Responses are 42-65 words, informal and shaped by the prompt's register instructions; hidden-reasoning token use ranged 614-1779 against the 2048 limit.
8. **Not blind / not held-out.** The persona definitions and prompts were designed by people who had seen survey summaries; the Gemini comparison involved no model selection but is not a held-out validation.
9. **Convenience sample.** The human sample is mostly young students.
10. The survey does not measure or validate all UTAUT2 constructs, and nothing here claims it does.

## 7. What can and cannot be concluded
- **Observed (preliminary):** the pipeline runs end to end on the available data; SSR scores on human Q26 text show a weak-to-modest positive rank association with Q21; for P1 and P2 the Gemini v2 mean expected ratings differ from the human means by -0.13 to 0.07 across the three encoders, with intervals as reported above. These are observations about 10 responses per persona, not demonstrated agreement.
- **Not supported:** that synthetic answers replicate human answers, that personas are validated, that results hold for P3/P4, that any encoder is best in general, or that the pipeline reproduces the published SSR method.
